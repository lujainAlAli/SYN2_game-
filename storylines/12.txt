The meeting opens with boring agenda items. Then the data center comes up.
Council member: "Silicon Valley Power brings in about 13% of our General Fund, roughly $45 million this fiscal year, making it our 3rd-largest revenue source. And about 77% of that, around $35 million, comes from data centers. We've managed data center growth responsibly for decades, with environmental review, disclosure requirements, and recycled water mandated for cooling. (City of Santa Clara, 2026)"
Company rep: "We'll follow all city requirements. We expect jobs, investment, and tax revenue, and we'll use efficient cooling."
Environmental scientist: "Santa Clara does better than most. Data centers here disclose their cooling systems, water type, and projected water use during environmental review. But the city has no cap on the number of data centers, and nothing in law guarantees residents come before revenue."
Public comment: Some residents support it, citing the city's track record. Others ask whether tax revenue will be prioritized over residents.
