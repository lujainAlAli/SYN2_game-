Paul Avenue AI Data Center Comprehensive Monitoring Initiative

{b}PILOT PROGRAM PROPOSAL JUNE 2025{/b}

1. Project Overview

The Hunters Point Biomonitoring Foundation proposes a one-year pilot program to establish a comprehensive environmental and human biomonitoring system for the Paul Avenue AI Data Center Campus in South Central Bayview-Hunters Point, San Francisco.

The proposed initiative would adapt the community exposure research model developed through the Hunters Point Community Biomonitoring Program to evaluate potential environmental exposures associated with high-density AI data center infrastructure.

The project would focus on the campus located between 200 and 400 Paul Avenue, which is situated near residential properties, public transportation, and senior housing.

2. Pilot Program Objectives

The proposed one-year initiative would pursue five primary objectives:

Develop a Conceptual Site Model (CSM) identifying potential contaminant sources, exposure pathways, and nearby sensitive populations.
Establish a one-mile monitoring zone centered around the Paul Avenue/Exeter residential intersection.
Install environmental monitoring equipment around the three-block campus perimeter.
Conduct voluntary human biomonitoring among residents and workers within the target area.
Establish a baseline environmental database to support ongoing community health research.
3. Environmental Monitoring

The initiative proposes monitoring five environmental categories:

Air quality: Particulate matter, nitrogen dioxide, sulfur dioxide, carbon monoxide, ozone, lead, and volatile organic compounds.
Soil: Selected heavy metals, petroleum hydrocarbons, and other potential legacy contaminants.
Noise: Continuous decibel measurements to distinguish facility-generated noise from Caltrain and neighborhood transit activity.
Temperature: Monitoring localized heat conditions around the facility.
Built environment: Assessment of potential exposure pathways involving buildings, utility corridors, groundwater, and surrounding infrastructure.
4. Human Biomonitoring

Residents and workers within the proposed one-mile target zone would be invited to participate voluntarily in urinary biomonitoring.

The proposal identifies the Mosaic Diagnostics EnviroTOX Complete + Metals test as a potential laboratory testing option.

Testing would evaluate selected biomarkers associated with environmental chemicals, volatile organic compounds, metals, and other substances.

Individual results would remain confidential, with aggregated findings used for community research.
