--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------SAN FRANCISCO, Calif. — Bayview-Hunters Point could become the site of another major data center development as demand for artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and digital infrastructure continues to grow. The proposed project, located along Paul Avenue, would add to an area that already hosts two data center facilities.

These figures were identified through private-market data companies and industry research rather than a comprehensive city-maintained inventory.

In fact, San Francisco's Planning Department has acknowledged that it does not formally track the number of data centers operating within city limits. Instead, these facilities have historically been classified under broader land-use categories, making it difficult to establish an official count.

Bayview-Hunters Point has a long history of industrial activity, making its industrial corridors attractive for large-scale infrastructure developments. However, the growing concentration of data centers has raised questions about electricity demand, environmental impacts, noise, and the distribution of industrial development within the neighborhood.

Public Announcement Date: May 25, 2025
