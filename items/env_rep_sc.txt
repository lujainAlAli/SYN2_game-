{b}Environmental Report: Santa Clara Data Centers{/b}

April 2026

Santa Clara is one of the largest data center markets in the United States, with significant electricity demand driven by cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and digital infrastructure.

Energy Usage: Electricity demand is relatively well documented through Silicon Valley Power, the city's municipal utility. However, actual electricity consumption is not consistently disclosed for every individual facility.

Projected Water Usage: Some development proposals include estimates of projected water consumption based on facility size and cooling requirements.

Actual Water Usage and Sources: Despite projected estimates, actual annual water consumption and specific water sources are not consistently disclosed at the facility level. Public information does not provide a comprehensive breakdown of potable versus recycled water usage.

Conclusion: Santa Clara provides relatively substantial information about data center electricity demand, but significant gaps remain in reporting actual water consumption and water sourcing (next10,2024).
