Location rules: restrict where developers can build based on groundwater risk, air pollution, and community approval.
Efficiency: server and storage virtualization runs multiple virtual servers on one physical machine, meaning fewer servers, lower energy, and less space.
Cooling: traditional air cooling uses AC, fans, and vents. Free cooling uses outside air or water to cut energy use. Liquid cooling uses water or coolants and is more efficient than air (Chien, 2025) .
Smart controls: real-time monitoring catches wasted energy. AI systems like SustainDC can delay flexible workloads, like backups, until the grid is cleaner, charge batteries off-peak, and manage cooling (Naug et al,2025).
Accountability: tracking specific and measarubale environmental sustainability metrics like PUE, WUE, ERR.
  PUE: Energy efficiency metric, Compare the energy consumption of computing applications and infrastructure 
  equipment with the energy consumption of IT equipment.
  WUE:Measure the data centre’s water efficiency.
  ERR:Measure the percentage of electronic waste generated by the data centre that is recycled or properly            disposed of, reducing environmental pollution (Murino et al, 2023).
Disclosure: strict public reporting of energy, water, and emissions.
